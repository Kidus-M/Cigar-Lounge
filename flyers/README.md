# Wolf Den Lounge event flyers

This folder contains five recurring-event flyers based on the titles, descriptions, and event images in `pages/events/index.js`.

## Print specification

- Finished size: 5 × 8 inches (12.7 × 20.32 cm), portrait
- Raster size: 1500 × 2400 px
- JPEG metadata: 300 DPI, maximum-quality export
- PDF page size: exactly 5 × 8 inches
- QR source: `wolf-den-events-qr.svg`, placed without changing its modules or quiet zone
- Safe area: primary copy and QR are inset from the trim edge

## Deliverables

- `jpg/`: print-ready 300 DPI JPEGs
- `png/`: lossless 1500 × 2400 PNGs
- `pdf/`: individual PDFs plus a combined five-page PDF
- `masters/`: editable SVG masters
- `assets/`: local copies of the exact website images and QR artwork used by the masters
- `previews/`: a small review sheet

The five flyers are the recurring Tuesday-through-Saturday events. The separate “Birthday Celebration” entry from the website is not included because the requested quantity was five.

For commercial printing, ask the print shop whether they require bleed. These files are supplied at the requested finished size without added bleed.
